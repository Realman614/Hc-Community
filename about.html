One School Network

Instead of information being scattered across different chats and platforms, HC-Community brings school life into one organized space.

NOT JUST ONE INTEREST

HC-Community belongs to the whole school.

Basketball, fencing and math were some of the first ideas behind the project, but they are only examples. HC-Community is designed for sports, academics, art, music, technology, photography, study groups, clubs, projects and communities that students create in the future.

HOW IT WORKS

Communities keep school conversations organized.

Students discover communities that interest them and join the ones they care about. Each community can contain different channels for its own conversations, while the Home page brings together updates from the communities each student has joined.

Communities Spaces built around a shared activity, subject or interest.
Channels Organized conversations inside each community.
School-wide Events and discovery connect individual communities back to the whole school.

CURRENT PROJECT

Built by a student. Growing into a real platform.

HC-Community now uses Firebase Authentication for verified accounts and Cloud Firestore for shared profiles, communities, memberships, posts, replies and likes. The platform is still pre-release, with moderation tools, administration features and additional production safeguards planned before the first school release.

THE GOAL

Useful enough to become part of everyday school life.

HC-Community should make it easier to discover opportunities, communicate with people who share your interests and understand what is happening around school without searching through disconnected group chats.

Simple Students should understand where everything is without needing a guide.
Organized Communities and channels keep different conversations from becoming one giant feed.
School-focused The platform is designed around the needs of a school community rather than the entire internet.
